Marta Smaldone is a visual artist and photographer based in Barcelona. Her work explores themes of memory, identity, and the interplay between personal and collective experience.

Through photography and video, she investigates how memory serves not as an anchor but as a catapult — a starting point for self-reflection and understanding identity as something fluid and evolving rather than fixed.
