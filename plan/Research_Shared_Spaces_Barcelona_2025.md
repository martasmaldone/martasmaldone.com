## Hybrid Gallery/Residency Spaces

These models combine multiple functions - relevant for Marta Ventures' integrated approach.

| Space | Location | Model |
|-------|----------|-------|
| [Chiquita Room](https://chiquitaroom.com) | Sant Antoni | Gallery + residency + publisher + bookshop |
| [Fase](https://frikifish.com/independent-art-spaces-in-barcelona/) | L'Hospitalet | Studio + exhibition + workshop space |
| [Trama 34](https://frikifish.com/independent-art-spaces-in-barcelona/) | L'Hospitalet | Shared workshop for mid-career artists |
| [Bombon Projects](https://bombonprojects.com) | - | Multipurpose gallery, non-conventional |

### Chiquita Room Model (Reference)
Located in Sant Antoni, functions as:
- Gallery
- Artist residency
- Cultural promoter
- Art book publisher
- "A place for sharing, for making art accessible"

This model closely aligns with Marta Ventures' vision of combining commerce + art + services.

## Key Neighborhoods for Art Spaces

### Poblenou
- Former industrial zone with reconverted warehouses
- Creative/tech hub
- Many coworking operators
- Spaces: Hangar, 2 Walls

### Raval
- Near MACBA
- Established artistic neighborhood
- Spaces: Unmaking Art, Compartir Espacios

### L'Hospitalet
- Emerging area, lower rents
- Growing gallery presence (Ana Mas Projects, NoguerasBlanchard)
- Spaces: Fase, Trama 34

### Sant Antoni
- Trendy, gentrifying
- Independent/alternative spaces
- Spaces: Chiquita Room

## Pricing Reference

| Type | Typical Cost |
|------|--------------|
| Temporary exhibition (hourly) | €100/hour |
| Coworking desk (monthly) | €150-300/month |
| Private studio (monthly) | €300-600/month |
| Pop-up shop (weekly) | €500-1500/week |
| Shared workshop space | €200-400/month |

*Prices vary significantly by location and amenities*

---

## Recommendations for Phase 4

1. **Start with pop-up/temporary** - Use Peerspace or Spathios to test locations before committing
2. **Consider L'Hospitalet** - Lower costs, growing art scene, good for emerging businesses
3. **Hybrid model** - Follow Chiquita Room approach: gallery + workshop + retail in one space
4. **Poblenou for lab** - If PROFESSIONALE approved, industrial spaces suit lab equipment

### Potential Partnership Opportunities
- La Lola Coworking - For workshops and exhibitions
- Chiquita Room - As distribution/consignment partner
- Hangar - For artist residency collaborations
